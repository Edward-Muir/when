| `eskimo-pie-patented` | fixed | description | Description has two errors. Christian Nelson was a candy shop owner in Onawa, Iowa, not Nebraska. The Eskimo Pie had no stick (Russell Stover removed it; the stick came with Good Humor), so 'ice cream on a stick' is wrong. Also the name says 'Patented' but the patent (US 1,404,539) issued 24 January 1922, while 1921 is the launch/naming; year 1921 conflicts with the name. Either year 1922 or name 'Eskimo Pie Is Launched' is needed. → Description: 'An Iowa shopkeeper coated ice cream bars in chocolate, creating a hand-held ice cream treat sold nationally.' Then set year 1922 (patent) to match the name, or rename to 'Eskimo Pie Is Launched' and keep 1921. | [link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eskimo_Pie) |
| `pavlova-created` | **unsupported** | year | Origin is disputed. 1926 is the year of Anna Pavlova's NZ tour; the earliest NZ recipes are 1927 (a jelly) and 1928-29 (meringue); the Perth claim is 3 October 1935 (Bert Sachse). No source dates the meringue dessert to 1926 firmly. Description's 'Australasia' hedges well. | [link](https://www.nzgeo.com/stories/from-russia-with-love/) |
