| `fredonia-natural-gas-well` | **unsupported** | year | Sources split. The Village of Fredonia and general references say William Hart's well was dug in 1821. AAPG and a Search and Discovery abstract call 1821 erroneous and date the first documented commercial well to 1825 (Fredonia Censor, Aug 31, 1825), with stores lit by August 1825. Both agree Fredonia is the origin of the US natural gas industry. | [link](https://www.aapg.org/?p=5029) |
| `oil-price-collapse-glut` | fixed | description | Year 1986 and the claim are correct: prices fell by more than 50 percent in the first half of 1986 after OPEC output rose. But 'the boom of the previous decade' is a relative dating phrase that hints at the era. → Producers flooded the market and crude lost more than half its value in months, ending years of high prices. | [link](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/lessons-from-the-1986-oil-price-collapse/) |
| `exxon-mobil-merger` | fixed | description | Year is right (announced December 1, 1998). But 'largest company in the world' is not supported: the AP said it would surpass GM, while CNN and the Canadian Encyclopedia call it the third-largest by market value; the consensus is that it became the largest oil company. → Two descendants of Standard Oil announced a union that would create the largest oil company in the world. | [link](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/exxon-and-mobil-to-merge) |
