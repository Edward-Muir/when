# App icon: Gemini prompts

The app icon is generated the same way as the card art: in the Gemini web app, in the house chiaroscuro oil-painting style (see `docs/events-images/index.md`). Four rounds of hand-drawn vector icons were rejected, because they could not match the painted cards.

The prompts below take the card style and tighten it for icons. They ask for a single object filling about 70% of the frame, a plain background and a bright rim light, so the image still reads at 60 px on a home screen.

## How to use

1. Paste one prompt into a fresh Gemini chat. Generate 2 or 3 images.
2. If one is close, reply in the same chat: "Make the object larger and simplify the background", or "Increase the contrast between the object and the background".
3. Download at full resolution and upload the images to the Claude session. The prompt number helps but is not required.
4. Claude crops each one to an iOS icon and tests it at 1024, 180, 60, 40 and 29 px before anything reaches the app.

## Prompts

### 1. Hourglass

```
A dramatic chiaroscuro oil painting designed as a mobile app icon. 1:1 square. ONE single object, centred, filling about 70% of the frame, with a bold, simple silhouette that is instantly recognisable even when shrunk to the size of a fingernail. Plain, softly graduated deep midnight-blue background with no scenery and no horizon. One warm light source from the upper left, deep shadows, and a bright rim of light separating the object from the background. Visible brushwork, but crisp edges on the object. A limited palette of two or three rich colours. No text, no letters, no numbers, no borders, no frame, no rounded corners, no vignette, no watermark. The subject: a brass hourglass standing upright, its sand glowing gold as it falls.
```
