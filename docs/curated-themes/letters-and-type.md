# The Letters & Type theme

A 30-card curated theme on printing, typing and typefaces, from the invention of paper to the
arrival of emoji. This note is why these 30 and not others.

## The scope rule

An event is in only if **it is a milestone in printing, typing or typefaces.**

- **Printing**: the first printed books, movable type, the presses and processes that
  mechanised it.
- **Typing**: the typewriter and the keyboard, from patent to the Selectric.
- **Typefaces and text encoding**: landmark faces, and the standards that put letters on screens.

Boundary: the look and mechanics of text, the Diamond Sutra and Jikji included. Secret writing
goes to Codes & Ciphers, Hangul to Korean Wave, reference books to Look It Up.

Edges argued by the reconciler:

- **`paper-invented` and `trajan-column`** are in as the surface and the letterform that printing
  and type grew from, reused as they stand.
- **`movable-type-china`** is the spine's Bi Sheng beat. The catalogue dates it 1040, the spine
  said 1041; it is the same event under a different convention, so the existing card was reused.
- **Linotype (1886) and the steam press (1814)** were cut as near-duplicates of existing cards
  at 1884 and 1811.
- **Times New Roman** is dated 1932, its first appearance in the newspaper. The spine's 1931 was
  the commission year.
- **Cut for crowding or weakness**: Subiaco, First Press in the Americas, Henry Mill, offset
  printing, Gill Sans, Adobe, Verdana, Calibri.

## Gates

Size 30, band 0 is 14 after blind grading (want 5+), no same-year pairs, 3 in 1985-1994
(limit 3), no card shared with any earlier theme. The spread is 7 of 8 bins (advisory).

## Fact-check flags

None are `unsupported` or `fix`. The checker searched only Times New Roman, the Bay Psalm Book
and Johnston; the other new cards rest on its own knowledge, so a reviewer should read their
years. The spine writer verified only five years by search (1465, 1530, 1605, 1816, 1904) and left
the rest to recall.
