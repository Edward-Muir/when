# The Crime & Punishment theme

A 30-card curated theme on policing and punishing crime, from the Code of Hammurabi to the
genealogy-database arrest of the Golden State Killer: police forces, prisons, methods of
execution and the science of identifying offenders. This note is why these 30 and not others.

## The scope rule

An event is in only if **it is a milestone in policing or punishing crime.**

- **Policing and detection**: a force founded, a detective branch, a way of identifying
  offenders (measurements, fingerprints, the polygraph, DNA), an emergency line.
- **Punishing**: a prison opened, a method of execution first used, a penalty abolished or a
  public punishment ended.

Edges, as the boundary names them:

- **Trials** go to Day in Court, so `miranda-v-arizona` is the only courtroom card, in because
  it governs how police question suspects. Invented detectives go to Whodunit and robberies to
  Stolen!, so neither appears.
- **`first-fleet-australia`** is in as the opening of transportation, punishment by exile.
- **`wireless-catches-crippen`** is a real arrest, not a fictional detective story, and the
  first to turn on wireless telegraphy.
- **`dna-evidence-convicts-killer`** and **`golden-state-killer-identified`** are in as the two
  steps of forensic DNA after the invention itself.

Twelve beats from the spine were cut to meet the gates, mostly from crowded stretches:
Statute of Winchester, Transportation Act, Panopticon, Elizabeth Fry, Auburn, New York police,
Elmira, the FBI laboratory, 999, Crime Stoppers, Three Strikes and the FBI fingerprint system.

## Gates

size 30, band 0 is 9 (after the blind grade), no same-year pairs, 3 cards in the busiest ten
years (1901-1910), and the largest overlap with an earlier theme is 1 card (great-fires,
day-in-court and code-of-life each share one). The "foothold" flags are the reconciler's own
judgement of fame-3 cards, not the gate's count. Several cards sit within a few years of each
other (1965/1966/1968 and 1921/1923/1924 are the closest); the gates allow it.

## Fact-check flags

- **`bertillon-criminal-measurement`** is unsupported on the year: sources give 1879 (devised),
  1882 (introduced) and 1883 (first used).
- Only that card was searched by the checker. The other 16 new cards were checked against the
  checker's own knowledge, not fresh searches.

## Intro

The popup this theme opens with (rules in [index.md](index.md#theme-intros)).
