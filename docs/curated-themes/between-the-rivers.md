# The Between the Rivers theme

A 30-card curated theme on Mesopotamia's three great powers, Sumer, Babylon and Assyria, from
the first settlement at Eridu to the fall of Babylon. This note is why these 30 and not others.

## The scope rule

An event is in only if **it is an event of Sumer, Babylon or Assyria.**

The boundary: Persia from Cyrus on goes to Kings of Kings, sieges to Under Siege, Egypt to
Pharaohs, and Ashurbanipal's library to Places of Learning.

Edges argued rather than assumed:

- **`fall-of-babylon-cyrus`** is in, and is also in Under Siege (an overlap of 1). It is the end of
  Babylonian rule, which closes the deck, even though Cyrus is the Persian side of it.
- **Esarhaddon's conquest of Egypt** was cut: it belongs to Pharaohs.
- **Nebuchadnezzar's first capture of Jerusalem** was cut: it is a siege, so it belongs to Under
  Siege, and it crowded the 600s BCE.
- **Hammurabi's destruction of Mari, Tukulti-Ninurta's conquest of Babylon, the Elamite theft of
  Hammurabi's stele, Nebuchadnezzar I, Tiglath-Pileser I, Qarqar and Dur-Sharrukin** were cut as
  obscure beats that thinned or crowded an Assyrian or Old Babylonian stretch.

## Gates

Size 30; band 0 25 after the blind grade (13 before it); no same-year pairs; at most 2 in any
10 years (-1800 to -1792); overlap 3 with rise-of-empires (`sargon-akkad-empire`,
`hammurabi-babylon-rise`, `nabopolassar-neo-babylonian-empire`) and 1 with sieges. Spread is
1 of 8 bins, which is advisory. Three close pairs are advisory only: 747 to 745 BCE, 612 to 605
BCE and 605 to 600 BCE.

## Fact-check flags still unsupported

- **`uruk-first-city-walled`**, description: the city wall is dated to about 3100 to 2900 BCE,
  not 3500 BCE, so "walled" is not supported at that year. The checker suggested dropping
  "walled" from the description and the slug.

Two evidence windows were set (`uruk-first-city-walled`, 3500 to 3100 BCE; `royal-tombs-of-ur`,
2600 to 2450 BCE). The detail prose writer reported claims it did not search; a reviewer should
spot-check the Eridu, Uruk, Urukagina, Enheduanna, Hammurabi, Hittite, Gilgamesh and Carchemish
entries.
